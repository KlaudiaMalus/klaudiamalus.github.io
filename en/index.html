Welcome to Kropka!

Learning a foreign language is much more than learning new words and grammar rules. It is also about discovering the culture, history, everyday customs and the way people see the world.

This is the approach I follow when teaching at Kropka — a place for learning Polish online.

I offer individual lessons in Polish as a foreign language, tailored to your level, needs and interests. I want learning to be effective, but also interesting — without stress or frustration.

Student-oriented and stress-free.

We work individually or in small groups, so during each lesson there is plenty of space for questions, conversation, revision and calmly developing the skills you need most.

Diverse materials.

During lessons, I use my own texts, recordings, interactive exercises and authentic materials. They make learning Polish more dynamic and engaging.

Flexible classes.

Lessons take place online, so you can learn from anywhere and fit your lessons more easily into your daily schedule and responsibilities.

Language and culture.

Learning a language also means getting to know the culture and everyday life of its speakers. That is why during lessons we talk about Polish customs and literature, and watch Polish films and TV series. This way, you learn not only how to speak Polish, but also how to better understand the context in which the language is used.

Whether you are just starting to learn Polish or you want to speak more freely and confidently — you are welcome at Kropka.
