Welcome to Kropka Language School!

Learning a foreign language is not only about acquiring new words and grammar – it is, above all, about discovering a new world, full of culture, traditions, and stories. At Kropka, we believe that language learning should not only be effective, but also open new horizons, while remaining inspiring and enjoyable.

Why choose us?

☕ Intimate atmosphere. We specialize in individual lessons and small groups (up to 6 people), which makes our classes friendly and personal. These are ideal conditions not only for learning, but also for making new connections and friends.

🤓 Passion and experience. Our teachers are inspired professionals who combine a love for their work with deep knowledge. We make sure that every class is not just about language, but also a meeting with a person who supports your growth and inspires you with a love for new words.

💡 Modern approach. We value variety – using multimedia, language games, interactive exercises, and original materials. Thanks to this, learning becomes dynamic, and you will notice your progress quickly.

🏃 Flexibility. Your time matters to us. That is why we offer both in-person and online classes, tailored to your schedule and lifestyle.

🤝 Cultural immersion. At Kropka, language meets culture. You will get to know Polish customs, history, and literature – not only in class, but also during workshops and cultural events regularly organized by the school.

Whether you are just beginning your journey with Polish or wish to deepen your skills – we warmly invite you to Kropka. Let us embark together on this fascinating journey through the labyrinth of language.

Join us and discover how beautiful the Polish language can be!
